# Plans & Pricing - User Guide

### Accessing Plans & Pricing

1. Open the app
2. Navigate to **Settings** (gear icon in the drawer/menu)
3. Tap on **"Plans & Pricing"** (card icon)
4. View available subscription plans

## How to Subscribe

### Step-by-Step

1. **Open Plans & Pricing**
   - Settings → Plans & Pricing

2. **Choose Your Plan**
   - Review the Free and Pro options
   - Compare features side-by-side

3. **Tap "Subscribe"**
   - On the plan you want (e.g., Pro)
   - If already on a plan, the button shows "Current Plan"

4. **Complete Purchase**
   - Follow App Store/Play Store prompts
   - Use Touch ID/Face ID or password
   - Confirm payment

5. **Enjoy Pro Features!**
   - Access unlocked immediately
   - Start using DALL-E image generation
   - Enjoy unlimited conversations

### Feature Status
- **✅ Green Check**: Feature included

## Managing Your Subscription

### View Current Plan
Your current plan is displayed in:
1. **Settings menu**: Under "Plans & Pricing" shows "Pro Plan Active" or "View available plans"
2. **Plans screen**: Badge on the active plan card

### Upgrading
To upgrade from Free to Pro:
1. Go to Plans & Pricing
2. Tap "Subscribe" on Pro plan
3. Complete purchase

### Canceling
To cancel your Pro subscription:
1. Go to device Settings
2. **iOS**: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Odyssey
3. **Android**: Play Store → Account → Subscriptions → Odyssey
4. Follow cancellation steps

**Note**: Access continues until end of billing period

### Restoring Purchases
If you reinstall the app or change devices:
1. The app automatically restores your subscription
2. Sign in with the same account
3. Your Pro status will be restored

## Pricing Details

### iOS (App Store)
- Pricing in your local currency
- Managed through Apple ID
- View in App Store account

### Android (Play Store)
- Pricing in your local currency
- Managed through Google account

### Billing
- **Monthly subscription** (default)
- Auto-renews unless cancelled
- Change or cancel anytime
- No refunds for partial months

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I try Pro before paying?
Check if a free trial is available during signup. Trial availability varies by region.

### What happens if I cancel?
You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period, then revert to Free plan.

### Can I change plans?
Yes, upgrade anytime. Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period.

### Is my payment secure?
Yes, all payments are processed by Apple/Google. We never see your payment information.

### Do I get charged immediately?
Yes, upon subscription confirmation. Charges appear on your App Store/Play Store account.

### Can I use Pro on multiple devices?
Yes, sign in with the same account on all your devices.

### What if I have issues?
Contact support at: support@odysseytech.ai

### Common Issues

**"Can't find my subscription"**
- Check Settings → Plans & Pricing
- Look for "Current Plan" badge
- Try "Restore Purchases" in App Store/Play Store

**"Payment failed"**
- Verify payment method in App Store/Play Store
- Ensure sufficient funds
- Check internet connection
- Try again later

**"Features not unlocked"**
- Close and reopen the app
- Check internet connection
- Wait a few minutes (processing time)
- Contact support if issue persists
